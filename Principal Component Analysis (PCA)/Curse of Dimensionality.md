**What's causing higher dimension ? : It's Sparsity**



Sparsity refers to the condition where most elements in a dataset, matrix, or model are zero or lack significant information.



* Imagine a big box of LEGO bricks, but most of the spaces inside are empty — only a few bricks are placed here and there. That’s called sparse — lots of empty spots.



&nbsp;	In machine learning, we sometimes use big tables (like spreadsheets) to hold numbers that describe things — but most of the numbers 	might be zero. For example, if we have a list of every word in the world, and we want to show which words are in your short 	sentence, most of the list will be zeroes, because only a few words are actually used. That’s sparsity!
