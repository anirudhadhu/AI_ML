# Outliers



Outliers is known as the data which is behaving completely different from other data in the particular row or column.



We have to know if the outliers and unwanted or is the very essential part of the data.



### 

### When is outliers dangerous ?



Check the vulnerability / importance of outlier in the dataset. Includes problem statement and nature of data.







### Effect of outliers in ML Algorithms



1. Liner Regression
2. Logistic Regression
3. AdaBoost
4. Deep learning



Here outliers create a major impact.



**Tree Based Algorithms:**



1. Decision Tree
2. Random Forest
3. Gradient Boosting



Here outliers may not create a major impact.





### **How to treat outliers ?**



1. Trimming (Removing)

&nbsp;	The major disadvantage is if the data is trimmed the dataset might get thinned and model cannot perform its best.

&nbsp;	The major advantage is its very fast



2\. Capping

&nbsp;	Putting the limit in outliers as placing a threshold bellow and above this is outlier.



3\. Treating as Missing Value 

4\. Discretization









### How to detect outliers ?



1. Normal Distribution
   
2. Skewed Distribution
   
3. Other Distribution







### Technique for outlier detection and removal



1. Z Score Treatment
2. IQR Based filtering
3. Percentile
4. Winsorizing
